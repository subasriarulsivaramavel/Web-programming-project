Essential Safety Tips

Simple habits can help you stay safer online.

🔗

Check Links Carefully

Fake websites can look almost identical to genuine ones.

  • Check the exact domain name.
  • Watch for misspellings.
  • Use known official website addresses.
🔐

Protect Your Passwords

Use strong account security to reduce unauthorized access.

  • Use unique passwords.
  • Consider a trusted password manager.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication.
📱

Never Share OTPs

Keep verification codes and financial credentials private.

  • Never share OTPs, PINs or CVVs.
  • Reject unknown login requests.
  • Contact your bank through official channels.
⏳

Beware of Urgency

Scammers may pressure you into acting without checking.

  • Question threats of account closure.
  • Verify urgent payment requests.
  • Pause before clicking unfamiliar links.
📧

Verify the Sender

A familiar display name does not prove who sent a message.

  • Check the full email address.
  • Confirm unusual requests separately.
  • Avoid unexpected attachments.
💳

Make Safe Payments

Take care when someone unexpectedly asks you to send money.

  • Verify the recipient before paying.
  • Never install remote-access apps on a stranger's request.
  • Question guaranteed-profit promises.

🚩 Common Scam Warning Signs

Stop and verify if you notice any of these warning signs.

● A message threatens account suspension unless you act immediately.
● A website has a misspelled or unfamiliar domain.
● Someone asks for your password, OTP, PIN or CVV.
● An unexpected prize requires an advance payment.
● A caller asks you to install an app or share your screen.
● Someone discourages you from contacting your bank directly.

🚨 Think You Have Been Scammed?

Take action quickly if you shared sensitive details or lost money.

  1. Contact your bank or payment provider immediately if money is involved.
  2. Change compromised passwords and secure your accounts.
  3. Keep transaction IDs, messages, phone numbers and screenshots.
  4. Report the incident through an official cybercrime channel.

In India, call 1930 to report financial cyber fraud. You can also submit a complaint through the official portal.
